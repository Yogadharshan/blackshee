# BLACKSHEE --- 2-HOUR AI HACKATHON GAME BRIEF

## One-line pitch

A tiny retro sheep RPG where you play the only black sheep in a world of
sheep, complete a collection mission, help weird sheep with sidequests,
discover secrets, and uncover a small emotional truth.

## Design target

Build a complete, playable web game in approximately 2 hours.

The game should feel like: - classic Pokémon / Game Boy-style
exploration - lightweight old-school Nintendo adventure design - absurd,
self-aware humor inspired by Undertale - cute on the surface,
unexpectedly sincere underneath

Do NOT attempt to build a full RPG.

## Player

The player is the BLACKSHEE.

Every character in the world is a sheep.

The player is visually distinct because they are black while the rest of
the sheep are white/light.

The black sheep is not inherently evil, cursed, powerful, or a chosen
hero. The identity difference is initially just a source of jokes and
social awkwardness.

## Core loop

Explore → talk to sheep → complete quests → collect Memories → unlock
final area → finish final encounter → ending.

## Main mission

Collect 5 Memories scattered around the world.

When all 5 Memories are collected: 1. the Old Shrine becomes accessible;
2. the player enters the final area; 3. the final encounter happens; 4.
the ending plays.

The final encounter does NOT need a complex combat system.

A short interaction, puzzle, dialogue sequence, or simple confrontation
is preferable if it produces a better finished game.

## Required systems

-   top-down player movement
-   collision
-   small connected world
-   NPC interaction
-   dialogue
-   main quest
-   3 small sidequests
-   5 collectible Memories
-   quest log / objective display
-   collectible counter
-   locked final area
-   final encounter
-   ending state
-   restart/replay capability

## Content philosophy

The world should be funny, but not every line should be a joke.

Approximate tone: - 70% normal cute RPG dialogue - 20% absurd / silly
dialogue - 10% strange / fourth-wall / unexpectedly sincere moments

Humor should make the player curious rather than constantly interrupting
gameplay.

## Secrets

Include at least 3 secrets if time allows.

1.  An obvious joke interaction.
2.  A repeated-interaction joke that escalates.
3.  A genuinely hidden room/object/NPC with an unusual reward or
    dialogue.

Secrets must never be required to finish the main mission.

## Emotional payoff

The game should begin silly and become quietly sincere.

Do not dump a long backstory.

Use: - collectibles - NPC dialogue - environmental clues - one final
reveal

The emotional beat should come from discovering something through play,
not from a long exposition scene.

## Scope rule

If a feature threatens the 2-hour deadline, remove it.

Priority order:

1.  Game launches.
2.  Player can move.
3.  Player can complete the main mission.
4.  Quests and collectibles work.
5.  Ending works.
6.  Humor and secrets.
7.  Visual polish.

Never sacrifice a working ending for extra features.
